Certificate validator (design demo)

Checks whether a certificate number was issued for a listed training activity. No names, e-mails or photos are stored on this site.

Optional: confirm the name on the certificate

The name is not stored on this site. If you hold the certificate, enter the name exactly as printed and the verification code printed beside the QR code; the check is computed in your browser and compared with a one-way fingerprint.

Need a copy of the certificate?

Certificates are held privately by the issuing office and are not downloadable from this site. The holder can request a re-issue by e-mail, quoting the certificate number; the office will send the PDF to the address on file for that participant.
